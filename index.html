The Beginning

The start of my relationship with chocolate can be said to have begun on May 6, 1990, when I was born. It was reportedly the first thing I tasted, when my older sibling, who had been rather miffed with me while I was preparing to enter this world, decided to extend an olive branch and share a small part of a chocolate bar with me.




Please note that all of this is fictional. I was not born on May 6, 1990.
